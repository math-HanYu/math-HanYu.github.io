How dependency-aware progress works

  • Leaf topics are the actual todo items. A volume is complete when all its topic leaves are checked.
  • With prerequisite locks enabled, a blocked volume cannot be checked until all of its direct prerequisite volumes are complete.
  • Dependencies are direct edges; transitive requirements are resolved automatically.
  • The 12-year path is the 40-hour calendar: Years 1–10 pair a 30 h/week mathematics quarter with a 10 h/week applied segment; Years 11–12 run the remaining applied segments as 40 h/week intensives.
  • Progress lives only in your browser’s local storage. Use Export to keep a portable JSON backup.
